Dr Cibin is a researcher in computational fluid dynamics and aerospace engineering with research interests in high-fidelity simulations, low-Reynolds-number aerodynamics, turbulence and transitional flows, numerical methods, and scientific computing. He completed postdoctoral research at Brigham Young University and the University of California, Davis.

## Research Interests

### High-Fidelity Computational Fluid Dynamics
Research focused on turbulence-resolving simulations, transitional flows, and advanced CFD methodologies for aerospace applications.

### Low-Reynolds-Number Aerodynamics
Investigation of low-Reynolds-number flow physics relevant to small-scale aerodynamic systems and biologically inspired flight.

### Numerical Methods and Scientific Computing
Development and application of computational techniques for complex fluid flow simulations and high-performance scientific computing.
